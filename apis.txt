LIVE APIS (called while the site runs)
--------------------------------------

1. NOAA Tides & Currents  (US government)
   Used for: water level now, tide forecast, storm surge
   https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/datagetter
   Docs: https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/
   Code: fetchTides()

2. Open-Meteo Forecast  (Swiss nonprofit)
   Used for: rain, temperature, humidity, wind, gusts, thunderstorms
   https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast
   Docs: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
   Code: fetchWeather()

3. Open-Meteo Air Quality  (Swiss nonprofit)
   Used for: air quality index (AQI), smoke (PM2.5), ozone
   https://air-quality-api.open-meteo.com/v1/air-quality
   Docs: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api
   Code: fetchAirQuality()

4. National Weather Service Alerts  (US government)
   Used for: active warnings, watches and advisories for NYC
   https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active?area=NY
   Docs: https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api
   Code: fetchAlerts()

5. NYC Open Data - 311 Service Requests  (NYC government)
   Used for: street flooding / sewer backup complaints
   https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/erm2-nwe9.json
   Docs: https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Social-Services/311-Service-Requests-from-2010-to-Present/erm2-nwe9
   Code: fetchRecent311(), fetch311History()

6. NYC Open Data - Hurricane Evacuation Zones  (NYC Emergency Management)
   Used for: the evacuation zone (1-6) of a checked address
   https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/epne-qv9x.json
   Docs: https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/epne-qv9x
   Code: fetchEvacZone()

7. NYC GeoSearch  (NYC Planning Labs)
   Used for: address -> latitude/longitude, and clicked point -> address
   https://geosearch.planninglabs.nyc/v2/search
   https://geosearch.planninglabs.nyc/v2/reverse
   Docs: https://geosearch.planninglabs.nyc/
   Code: geocode(), reverseGeocode()

8. FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer  (US government)
   Used for: official flood zone at an address, and the flood-zone map layer
   https://hazards.fema.gov/arcgis/rest/services/public/NFHL/MapServer/28/query
   https://hazards.fema.gov/arcgis/rest/services/public/NFHL/MapServer/export
   Docs: https://hazards.fema.gov/femaportal/wps/portal/NFHLWMS
   Code: fetchFemaZone(), makeFemaLayer()

9. OpenStreetMap tiles  (OpenStreetMap volunteers)
   Used for: the map background pictures
   https://tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png
   Backup: https://{s}.tile.openstreetmap.fr/hot/{z}/{x}/{y}.png
   Docs: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
   Code: addBasemap()


DATA FILES (made once, saved in data/)
--------------------------------------

10. data/nyc-ntas.geojson - the 195 neighborhood shapes
    From: NYC Department of City Planning, via the NYC Health Department
    https://github.com/nycehs/NYC_geography
    Made by: scripts/build/build-geodata.mjs

11. data/nyc-elevation.png - ground height for all of NYC, stored as pixel colors
    From: AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS 3D Elevation Program)
    https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
    Made by: scripts/build/bake-elevation.mjs


NUMBERS TYPED IN BY HAND (no API)
---------------------------------
- Neighborhood ratings in js/facts/config.js: simplified from FEMA flood maps,
  NYC DEP stormwater maps, the NYC Heat Vulnerability Index and the
  NYC Environment & Health Data Portal (https://a816-dohbesp.nyc.gov/IndicatorPublic/)
- Sea level rise projections: NYC Panel on Climate Change (NPCC 2015, 2019)
- Historical replays in js/models/scenarios.js: rebuilt from NOAA, NWS and EPA records
